What it is

A grand European roulette table in plain HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio and vanilla JavaScript: a big wheel in perspective whose ball is physically simulated on the track, down the cone past the deflectors and across the frets; a real human dealer in a live-stream panel who speaks every call with generated voice and lip-synced video; three regulars who bet alongside you; and a board that tracks the last twenty numbers.

Features

The wheel

Rotor and ball spin in opposite directions; the ball decays with friction, drops, strikes the deflectors, hops over the frets with decaying bounces and settles in the predetermined pocket. The camera pushes in for the drop.

The dealer

Generated portrait and voice, lip-synced talking-head clips for the table phrases, every number called with its colour, winners congratulated by name, captions under the video.

The table

Full European layout with 157 bet spots: straights, splits, streets, corners, six lines, trios, first four, columns, dozens and the even-money bets, snapping to the nearest spot. Limits 1–500 inside, 2,500 outside.

The regulars

Sophia plays the outside, Marcus chases numbers, Elena mixes it up. They place chips during the window, keep bankrolls and rebuy when broke.

The board

Last twenty numbers as coloured tokens, newest first, with red/black/zero, odd/even and hot-number counts, plus the betting countdown.

Sound

Salon murmur, the ball's roll rising and falling with speed, deflector clicks, fret hops, the settle rattle, chips, the no-more-bets chime and win fanfares, all synthesized live.

Run it yourself

Serve the folder (python -m http.server 8771 or start.cmd on Windows) and open http://localhost:8771/. The README covers configuration, the house-edge check and the headless screenshot tool.
